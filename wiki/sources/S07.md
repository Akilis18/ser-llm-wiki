## Role and evidence limits

The review describes its literature selection in §1.1 (p. 4) and compares work spanning different datasets and methods. Its conclusions about child SER are a literature synthesis. Child-BESD totals are internally inconsistent: §4.2/Table 3 and §8.1 give 4,320, while §7/Table 5 gives 4,200. The Wiki omits the count pending the corpus paper.
