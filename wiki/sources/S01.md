## Role and evidence limits

The author frames this as an audio-focused review organized around concepts and motivation, with a selective discussion of prior work (§1, p. 1). Treat the reviewed literature as background synthesis and the suggested feature design in §9 as a proposal. The Wiki uses this source for introductory concepts and preserves original-paper and dataset-specific questions for later ingests.
