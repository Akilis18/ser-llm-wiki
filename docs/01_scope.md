# Scope and teaching contract

## Title
AI 如何聽出情緒？從聲音特徵到深度學習的語音情緒辨識入門

## Audience
Beginners with no prior speech-processing knowledge; familiar with basic computers and everyday AI concepts.

## Learning objectives
1. Distinguish Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) from Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR).
2. Explain what waveform, sample rate, spectrogram, pitch, energy and MFCC represent.
3. Compare categorical emotions with dimensional Valence–Arousal.
4. Describe a traditional features + classifier pipeline and a pretrained encoder pipeline.
5. Identify dataset/split considerations and interpret accuracy, UAR and F1.
6. Explain uncertainty, speaker variation, noise, domain shift and privacy limitations.

## Wiki chapters
1. SER introduction
2. Acoustic signals and features
3. Emotion representation
4. Machine learning and pretrained models
5. Datasets and evaluation
6. Applications, limitations and ethics

## Scope constraints
- Speech acoustics only: no multimodal fusion in depth.
- No model training needed for course deliverable.
- Do not assert that loudness/pitch directly reveal an emotion in isolation.
- Use 7 selected scholarly sources (at least 5 actually ingested).

## Ingestion approval checklist
Before creating a page from a source: confirm full text acquired, metadata verified, intended chapter mapping explained, and user agrees on scope.
